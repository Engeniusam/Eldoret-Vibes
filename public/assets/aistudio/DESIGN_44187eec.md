# Design System Document

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a **Corporate / Modern** style, characterized by a reliable, balanced, and highly professional aesthetic inspired by IBM's design principles. The interface emphasizes clarity, functional precision, and structured layouts to instill confidence and trust in enterprise users.

## Colors
The color palette is built for clarity and accessibility, utilizing a commanding primary blue (`#0f62fe`) for primary actions and focus states, balanced by a sophisticated neutral gray (`#525252`) and secondary gray (`#6f6f6f`). A purposeful tertiary green (`#198038`) highlights success states and positive indicators.

## Typography
The typography relies entirely on **IBM Plex Sans**, delivering an industrial, clean, and legible reading experience across all viewports. Type hierarchies are strictly maintained to guide the user's eye efficiently through dense data and administrative workflows.

## Layout & Spacing
A reliable fluid grid system with consistent 16px gutters and 24px outer margins governs the layout. Spacing scales use a predictable rhythm to establish clear whitespace hierarchies between form controls, containers, and data tables.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is primarily communicated through subtle tonal surface variations and low-contrast outlines rather than heavy drop shadows, aligning with a clean, modern enterprise interface standard.

## Shapes
The design system uses a **Soft** roundedness level (0.25rem base border radius for standard components, scaling up to 0.5rem and 0.75rem for larger containers), striking a balance between approachable modern form and structural precision.

## Components
Components are engineered for high-density enterprise use. Buttons feature crisp borders with soft rounded corners, input fields prioritize clear focus states using the primary blue, and data display cards rely on clean structural borders and structured typography.
